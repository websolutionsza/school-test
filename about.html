About Our School

Discover our mission, vision, and the values that guide our learners every day.

Welcome to Greenwood High

Greenwood High School is a place where educational excellence meets community spirit. We are committed to providing a supportive and stimulating environment where every learner is encouraged to achieve their full potential.

Our dedicated staff work to ensure that learners receive a well-rounded education that prepares them for the challenges of the future. We foster a culture of respect, integrity, and continuous learning — principles that shape daily life at the school.

What guides us

Our Core Values

The principles that shape how we teach, learn, and grow together.

01

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in everything we do — from academics to extracurricular activities — encouraging learners to reach their personal best.

02

Integrity

We uphold honesty, ethics, and strong moral principles. Doing the right thing is fundamental to building strong character.

03

Community

We foster a sense of belonging and mutual respect. Learners, parents, and staff work together to create a supportive, inclusive environment.

04

Discipline

We promote self-discipline and responsibility. A structured environment is essential for effective learning and personal growth.

Our approach to learning

Greenwood combines a structured academic programme with opportunities for creative, physical, and personal development. Learners are encouraged to ask questions, take responsibility for their progress, and contribute meaningfully to school life.

Parents are regarded as partners in the educational journey. Open communication between the school office, teachers, and families is central to how we operate.
