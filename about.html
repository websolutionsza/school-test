About Our School

Discover our mission, vision, and the values that guide our learners every day.

Welcome to Greenwood High

Greenwood High School is a place where educational excellence meets community spirit. We are committed to providing a supportive and stimulating environment where every learner is encouraged to achieve their full potential.

Our dedicated staff and modern facilities ensure that learners receive a well-rounded education that prepares them for the challenges of the future. We believe in fostering a culture of respect, integrity, and continuous learning.

Our Core Values

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in everything we do, from academics to extracurricular activities, encouraging learners to reach their personal best.

Integrity

We uphold honesty, ethics, and strong moral principles. We believe that doing the right thing is fundamental to building strong character.

Community

We foster a sense of belonging and mutual respect. By working together, learners, parents, and staff create a supportive and inclusive environment.

Discipline

We promote self-discipline and responsibility. We believe that a structured environment is essential for effective learning and personal growth.
